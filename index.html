About and privacy

Shekel Clicker is a fan-made idle game inspired by Orteil's Cookie Clicker. Your save lives only in this browser's local storage; the game sends nothing anywhere and loads no outside scripts, fonts, or trackers. Hava Nagila is a traditional folk melody, first published in 1922; the arrangement here is synthesized live in your browser. Pixel art drawn for this game. Fonts: Pixelify Sans and Frank Ruhl Libre (SIL Open Font License).
